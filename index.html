How to play

  1. 1Panther moves first. Rings mark your legal squares.
  2. 2Trap a straight line of your opponent’s discs between the disc you place and one of yours. Everything trapped flips.
  3. 3No legal move means you pass. When neither side can move, the most discs wins.
  4. 4Corners can never be flipped back. The squares touching a corner are how you lose one.
